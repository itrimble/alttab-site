Switcher modes

Choose the level of visual detail you want.

Use compact app icons when you want speed, titles when you need clarity, or ScreenCaptureKit thumbnails with grouped per-window selection when visual context matters.

Window previews when Screen Recording is enabled, with nested targets for apps that have multiple windows.

Clean-room boundary

Independent code, assets, and product language.

Window Switcher Pro is being built as its own macOS utility. The project avoids copied source, private APIs, inherited branding, and unsupported update assumptions.

Public APIsAccessibility, AppKit, Carbon hotkeys, ServiceManagement, and ScreenCaptureKit.
